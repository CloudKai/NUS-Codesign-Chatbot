## Behavior and acceptance

- Additive `project_resume` on owned GET learning-state projects exact active,
  stage-tagged student contributions; explicit lecture help is excluded. Untagged
  legacy text remains readable in Chat and is never guessed into a stage.
- Active stage and expanded stage previews show the latest contribution, earlier
  contributions, separately labelled coach feedback/completion summaries, saved
  recommendation status, and one deterministic stage action. Pending actions point
  to confirmation; completed-stage actions invite refinement/return. Full feedback
  and existing Review history are retained; no page-load model/review job call.
- Browser-tab sessionStorage text drafts are keyed by opaque owner/notebook scope.
  Matching widget identity and scoped DOM recovery prevent cross-notebook drafts.
  Text survives reload, confirmation, stage revisits, and A/B notebook switches.
  New chat is isolated; sending restored text clears it and saves the contribution.
- No schema migration, reset, or existing student notebook/database/file writes.
  No retroactive awards. Canonical revision edits replace superseded contributions
  in the resume view; historical underlying transcript/revisions remain untouched.

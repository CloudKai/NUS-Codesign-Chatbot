`answer_delivered` means a new persisted nonempty chat answer; it does not prove
browser receipt or usefulness. Replays, Deep Review and checkpoints do not inflate
it. Useful-answer assessment is null until independently supplied with evidence.
Session usage is explicitly a hashed notebook/UTC-day proxy, not people or actual
learning-session duration. Random event/operation ids support correlation;
raw notebook/owner ids, prompts, student text, source content, credentials and
exception text are excluded from new accounting events. Checkpoint success is
recorded only after durable completion, and Deep Review reads its saved status.

The log stream is an exportable operational ledger, not an exactly-once durable
invoice. Hidden model/SDK retries and missing exports require separately authorized
billing reconciliation. Complete generation estimates are suppressed for unknown
retry usage or incomplete events. Old runtimes and uninstrumented provider
adapters remain compatible with unknown meters. Mock meters are explicit zero.

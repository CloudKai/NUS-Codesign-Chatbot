## Characterization and compatibility decision

Existing journeys store focus in `current_stage` independently of explicit
`completed_stages` and `stage_notes`; transition decisions live on transcript
rows and append-only revisions preserve historical rows. Previously, selection
mode recorded completion on a provider ADVANCE before confirmation, hid the
confirm/reject controls, and allowed a pending destination as a selection.
HMW application guards required literal wording and promoted even vague or
solution-locked formal templates over a semantic STAY.

No migration or retroactive completion inference is needed. Preserve existing
focus, notes, explicit completion records, and transcript text exactly. Legacy
non-contiguous completions remain revisitable; the first missing frontier is
reachable, but neither a later focus nor a gap unlocks later unfinished stages.
A legacy already-completed stage can be selected/left freely; a remaining legacy
pending may be confirmed once or rejected. Reapplying a completion never replaces
its original note. No existing student database or uploaded file was modified.

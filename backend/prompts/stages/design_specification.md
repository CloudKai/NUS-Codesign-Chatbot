STAGE: DESIGN SPECIFICATION
PURPOSE: Make the selected design's requirements, behavior, and constraints explicit.

Help the student turn a promising concept into a specification that another
person could understand, test, and critique.

Guide the student to state:

- intended users, use context, and core behavior
- functional and non-functional requirements
- constraints, dependencies, and important assumptions
- success criteria or observable outcomes
- rationale for consequential design choices
- relevant fairness, privacy, transparency, non-maleficence, or responsibility considerations

ADVANCE when the core behavior, important requirements, constraints, and
success criteria are explicit enough for deep analysis.

STAY when the design remains an aspiration, key behavior is ambiguous, or
there is no basis for evaluating success.
